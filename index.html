Privacy Policy for LifeDesk

1 Core Philosophy & Overview

Welcome to LifeDesk. We believe personal finances, contracts, and passwords should remain completely private. Unlike traditional cloud services, LifeDesk is engineered with an offline-first, zero-knowledge architecture.

  • We operate zero developer servers to collect your personal records.
  • We do not profile, track, or sell your information.
  • All records stay exclusively on your device, with optional encrypted sync to your private Google Drive.

2 Information Handled by LifeDesk

All data is created directly by you or imported with your explicit permission:

Data Type Processing Location Security & Access
Financial Transactions & Accounts 100% On-Device (SQLite) Protected within Android's private app sandbox.
Documents, Receipts & PDFs Device internal storage (filesDir/documents/) Accessed only via secure FileProvider.
Password Vault Items On-Device Encrypted (AES-256-GCM) Derived using PBKDF2 (100,000 rounds). Developers cannot decrypt.
Smart Paste (Clipboard) Temporary RAM memory Parsed locally with regex. Copied passwords cleared after 30s.

3 Google Drive Cloud Backup (Optional)

If you choose to enable cloud backups in Settings, LifeDesk communicates with Google Drive under the following strict conditions:

  • Restricted Scope (drive.appdata): LifeDesk cannot view, modify, or access any personal files in your regular Google Drive. Access is restricted strictly to an isolated app folder.
  • Client-Side Encryption: All backup files are encrypted with your master passphrase using AES-256-GCM before upload. Google cannot inspect your financial records or passwords.
  • OAuth 2.0: We do not see or store your Google password.

4 Tracking & Third-Party SDKs

LifeDesk strictly excludes commercial telemetry and ad networks:

  • No Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics
  • No Meta / Facebook Tracking SDKs
  • No Advertising Frameworks (AdMob, Unity, AppLovin)
  • No Background Location Telemetry

5 User Rights & Data Erasure

Under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy standards, you maintain absolute data ownership:

  • Export Anytime: Export transactions to CSV spreadsheet or download your complete encrypted JSON backup.
  • Instant Data Erasure: Tap Settings > Clear All Data inside the app to irreversibly delete all local databases and files.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling LifeDesk immediately wipes all application databases and document files from your device.
